The website is not bad. In fact, the structure is already quite good. The problem is that it currently looks like a customized Elementor/Blocksy template, rather than the website of a distinguished retired naval commander, historian, and public intellectual.

The difference is mostly in visual hierarchy, typography, imagery, spacing, content presentation, and restraint.

Right now it says:

"I customized a portfolio template."

We want it to say:

"This is the official digital home of a serious Nigerian public intellectual."

And you can absolutely achieve that with Elementor + free tools.

1. The biggest problem: everything has the same visual weight

Look at your current homepage.

You have:

Areas of Expertise → Featured Articles → Professional Journey → Latest Commentary → Invite to Speak

That's structurally fine.

But visually, almost every section feels like another collection of boxes.

That's what is making it feel "template-ish."

For this man, I would introduce a much stronger editorial hierarchy.

Think:

PERSON → IDEAS → CREDIBILITY → WRITING → ENGAGEMENT

Rather than:

SECTION → CARDS → SECTION → CARDS → SECTION → CARDS
